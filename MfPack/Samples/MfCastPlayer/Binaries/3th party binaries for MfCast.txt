Needed 3th party binaries for MfCastPlayer:

Download yt-dlp from: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
Download ffMpeg from: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

Install:

unpack yt-dlp_win_x86.zip.
Copy yt-dlp_x86.exe to: ..\MfCastPlayer\Binaries
Copy folder _internal and subfolders to: ..\MfCastPlayer\Binaries

unpack ffMpeg.zip
Copy ffMpeg.exe to: ..\MfCastPlayer\Binaries

Folder structure:

MfCastPlayer
|
|__Binaries
     |
     |_ _internal
